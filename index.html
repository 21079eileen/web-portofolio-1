Our Story

Meet Eileen

I'm Eileen, a high school student with a dual passion: crafting the perfect flaky pastry and writing clean HTML/CSS. This bakery is a conceptual project where I blend my love for artisanal baking with my journey into web development.

Every design choice here reflects the warmth and care I'd want in my own real-world shop—inviting cream tones, earthy terracotta accents, and a focus on quality.
